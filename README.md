# Privacy Policy — 101 Slovenian Conversation  
**Last Updated: October 11, 2026*

## Introduction

Welcome to 101 Slovenian Conversation (“we,” “our,” or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, disclosed, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application (the “App”).

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

You do not need to create an account or provide personal information to use the App. The App may store the following information locally on your device:

- **Saved vocabulary:** Slovenian words and phrases you save, with English translations.
- **Highlights:** Text you highlight in conversations, including Slovenian and English text.
- **Completed conversations:** Conversations you mark as completed.
- **Optional profile details:** Such as a display name, if this feature is available and you choose to use it.

This information is stored on your device and is not sent to our servers, unless a feature or service described in a later version of the App changes how it works. The App provides Slovenian content with English translations to support English-speaking learners.

### Information Collected Automatically

The App or its service providers may automatically collect information such as:

- **Device information:** Device type, operating system version, and device identifiers.
- **Usage data:** How you interact with the App, which features you use, and time spent in the App.
- **Performance data:** Crash reports, error data, and performance metrics.
